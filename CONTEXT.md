# Lirna

Lirna is Nathan's first-party personal environment for researching questions,
working with sources, developing durable knowledge, and practicing recall.

## Language

### Knowledge

**Owned note**:
A durable Markdown note whose substance Nathan has explicitly reviewed and accepted. Owned notes remain compatible with direct editing in Obsidian and Neovim.
_Avoid_: final note, AI note

**Draft**:
A provisional new note or revision that may be changed, split, rejected, or accepted before it affects an owned note.
_Avoid_: generated note, output

**Vault**:
The canonical collection of owned Markdown notes and their file-native relationships, currently at `~/vaults`.
_Avoid_: database, second brain

### Research and reading

**Research thread**:
A durable conversation that preserves questions, sourced answers, citations, selected context, and any drafts it produces.
_Avoid_: chat log, run, door record

**Source**:
Material Nathan studies or cites, including web pages, PDFs, EPUBs, audio, video, and existing Markdown.
_Avoid_: input, attachment

**Annotation**:
A durable comment or highlight anchored to a source or owned note. An annotation may remain useful without becoming a draft or owned note.
_Avoid_: clipping, temporary selection

### Learning

**Quiz**:
A contextual assessment of understanding. Quiz items do not automatically become scheduled repetition items.
_Avoid_: flashcard session, test

**Review**:
Nathan's deliberate examination of provisional knowledge or learning material. The object being reviewed must be named: draft review, quiz review, or spaced-repetition review.
_Avoid_: approval (unless acceptance is specifically meant)

**Visualization**:
A first-party visual representation used to explore relationships, navigate knowledge, or explain material through generated diagrams.
_Avoid_: decorative graph, mirror
